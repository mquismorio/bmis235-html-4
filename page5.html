New Year's

Our New Year's tradition also commenced in 2023! I guess that was our big year of new traditions...

Usually, New Year's is hosted at mi casa. Overall, it's pretty low pressure in terms of cooking compared to Friendsgiving and Christmas. We also stay in comfy clothes, play games, bake cookies, and sing karoke. One of my favorite parts of New Year's 2025 was hearing everyone's goals for the new year, and playing the "finish the song" game! Uno is also a certified classic with us, so we played that too...
