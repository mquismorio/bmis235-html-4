Thanksgiving to Friendsgiving

Our Friendsgiving tradition began in 2023.

The first one was more fun than we expected, and year after year we find ourselves looking forward to this celebration with greater anticipation than the last! We started this during our sophomore year in high school. This year will be our 4th year celebrating this fun holiday, and we're all second years in university. One standout highlight from Friendsgiving 2025 was playing our customized version of Jeopardy!.
